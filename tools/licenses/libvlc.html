libVLC

VLC plays media with libVLC 3, free software by VideoLAN and the VLC authors (videolan.org). libVLC and libVLCcore are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later, and loaded as separate libraries, which you may replace with your own build of the same version. Some of the plugins that come with it are under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, among them those built on x264, x265, FFmpeg's postproc, libdvdnav, FAAD2, libmad and libsidplay2; the others, and most of the libraries they include, such as FFmpeg's codecs, are under the LGPL or more permissive licenses.

The source code of libVLC, its plugins and the libraries they include is published by VideoLAN at download.videolan.org, under the version number this app reports in About VLC. The source code of this app is at github.com/ItayKarp/VLC-Remake-GPUI.
